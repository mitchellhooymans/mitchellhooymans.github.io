Workshop

How to Create a Website for Astrophysicists

Presented at the Quasar Collaboration meeting.

Show Abstract

In this workshop, I will be presenting an overview of web design, detailing the essential elements to include in an academic portfolio, as well as providing a template for you to get started right away. The session will focus on modern, low-maintenance tools that allow researchers to maintain a professional digital presence without a massive time investment. We will cover how to easily link your publications and highlight your work. No prior web design expertise is needed! This session is highly recommended for all students and early career researchers.
